Economist, writing about information and markets.

I'm Mardoqueo Arteaga, an economist at Meta and a contributor to The Honest Economist, where I write about AI, macroeconomics, and the information problems underneath both. My research studies how people form expectations: what households hear from central banks, what credit markets believe, and what gets lost in between.
