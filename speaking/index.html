Speaking

Mardoqueo Arteaga is an economist at Meta and a contributor to The Honest Economist. He speaks to professional and university audiences about what data work looks like from the inside, what AI is doing to labor markets, and how information actually moves through modern businesses.

Talk topics
Career building with data

What corporate experience actually teaches: how data science and analytics work inside large companies, and what early-career professionals should know. Aimed at data science conferences and student audiences.

The economics of AI and labor

Which jobs AI displaces first, which skills it reprices upward, and what the hiring data says about the entry-level bottleneck. Draws on his research and his column on AI and work.

Information in the wild

How information moves through marketing and big tech: what advertising actually does, why measurement keeps breaking, and what happens when the customer is an algorithm.
