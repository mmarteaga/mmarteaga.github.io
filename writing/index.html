What is The Honest Economist?

The Honest Economist is a publication started with my former colleague, Kent Bhupathi, where I am a contributor. I found the need to have a place where I can think about how information moves through an economy, what people actually hear when institutions speak, and where the textbook story breaks down.

These essays are where I work those questions out in public, in plainer language than a journal would allow. They reflect my personal opinions only, not those of my employer.
